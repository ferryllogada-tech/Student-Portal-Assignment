Student Services

The University of Eastern Africa, Baraton provides various services to support students in their academic, social, spiritual and personal development.

1. Library Services

The university library provides students with access to books, journals, research materials and other academic resources.

Visit the UEAB Official Website for more information.

2. Health Services

Students have access to health services to support their health and wellbeing while studying at the university.

3. Accommodation

The university provides residential facilities for students, allowing them to live within the university environment.

4. ICT Services

ICT services support students in accessing computers, internet facilities, online learning resources and other digital services.

5. Cafeteria Services

The university provides dining and cafeteria facilities where students can access meals and refreshments.

6. Sports and Recreation

Students can participate in sports and recreational activities that promote physical fitness, teamwork and social interaction.

7. Spiritual Services

Students have opportunities to participate in worship, spiritual activities and religious programmes.
