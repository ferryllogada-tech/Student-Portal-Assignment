About Us

University History

The University of Eastern Africa, Baraton (UEAB) is a Seventh-day Adventist institution of higher learning located in Baraton, Nandi County, Kenya.

The university was established in 1978 following the need for an Adventist university in East Africa. Classes began in September 1979 in temporary farm structures.

Our Mission

UEAB provides and advances a holistic quality Adventist education that develops men and women to be earnest seekers of truth and equips them with appropriate knowledge, skills, and attitudes for service to God and humanity.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of excellence in higher education and research, producing world-class professionals equipped with moral virtues.
